# CB MODULE — BANK OF JAPAN (Policy Board)

*Durable structure only. §8 always live. Personnel entries dated `[verify]`.*

## 1. Mandate & framework
Price stability target: 2% CPI (2013 joint statement with the government). Framework has
changed repeatedly and recently — QQE → NIRP → YCC → post-YCC normalization with rate
hikes and JGB-purchase reduction beginning 2024 `[current policy rate, JGB purchase plan,
and any framework language: verify — this is the fastest-moving framework among majors]`.
Historic context matters: decades of deflation psychology make the BoJ asymmetric —
institutionally more afraid of tightening too early than too late.

## 2. Committee mechanics
Policy Board: 9 members — Governor, 2 deputy governors, 6 members. Individual votes
published. Government representatives attend (non-voting) and can request delays —
fiscal-monetary coordination is unusually explicit. 8 meetings/year; Outlook Report
(forecasts) quarterly (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct).

## 3. Communication hierarchy (weight, descending)
1. Policy statement (release time is **variable** — a late statement historically hints
   at contested decisions) + vote split.
2. Governor presser (15:30 JST, in Japanese — see §7).
3. Outlook Report — the fan charts and the *risk-balance* language.
4. Summary of Opinions (~10 days after; unattributed) then full Minutes (much later —
   stale on arrival, skip for trading purposes).
5. Deputy-governor and member speeches to regional business leaders — historically the
   BoJ's preferred pre-signaling channel for policy changes.
6. **Nikkei / Jiji / NHK sourced stories pre-meeting** — the BoJ's de facto leak
   channel; a Nikkei "BoJ to consider X" piece on meeting eve has strong historical hit
   rate. Treat as quasi-communication, direction over detail.

## 4. Current-priority variables `[verify each use]`
Underlying inflation vs. the 2% target sustainably ("wage-price virtuous cycle"):
**Shunto** spring wage negotiations (Rengo tallies, Mar–Jul), services CPI, output gap,
inflation expectations surveys (Tankan), and JPY pass-through (a weak-yen inflation
overshoot is the modern trigger for hawkishness — historically inverted vs. the 20th-c.
BoJ). USDJPY level itself is an unofficial reaction-function input; MoF (not BoJ) owns
intervention — don't conflate them.

## 5. People — ALL entries `[verify before use]`
Governor/deputies/members and their published vote records: boj.or.jp/en. Vote records
are the lean baseline; academic-vs-bureaucrat background is a weak durable prior only.

## 6. Instruments to watch
- Policy-rate pricing: TONA OIS / `WIRP` (verify); euroyen legacy contracts illiquid — verify best listed proxy.
- JGB 2y/10y/30y: `GJGB2 / GJGB10 / GJGB30 Index` (verified); 10y is the YCC-legacy focal point.
- USDJPY `USDJPY Curncy`; JPY vs. rate differentials (US 10y spread) — the dominant macro cross.
- Nikkei/TOPIX for the risk-asset leg; JGB purchase-operation announcements (boj.or.jp
  operations calendar) for the QT leg.

## 7. Known traps — translation is a first-class risk here
- **Canonical text is Japanese.** English releases are official translations but the
  presser is Japanese with no official real-time English; wire translations flatten
  hedges. Japanese policy speech is built on calibrated ambiguity — constructions like
  "±することも考えられる" ("it is also conceivable that…") signal optionality, not
  intent; English wires often render them as near-commitments. Lower confidence one
  notch on any presser-derived nuance unless working from the Japanese.
- Vote-split + Summary of Opinions is the honest signal set; minutes arrive too late.
- Government presence: near elections or fiscal events, hawkish moves get delayed —
  check the political calendar.
- The regional-speech pre-signal pattern (§3.5) means "surprise" hikes are usually
  pre-signaled somewhere — before calling something a surprise, check the prior two
  weeks of member speeches and Nikkei.

## 8. STALE BOUNDARY — always pull live
Current policy rate and IOER structure; JGB purchase plan/QT pace; current board and
votes; latest Outlook Report projections; current Shunto tally (in season); USDJPY level
and MoF intervention posture. Sources: boj.or.jp/en, mof.go.jp (FX), BBG ECO.
