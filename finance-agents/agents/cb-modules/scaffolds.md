## SNB (Swiss National Bank)
1. **Mandate:** price stability = CPI 0–2%; explicit willingness to use FX intervention
   and negative rates as tools. 2. **Mechanics:** 3-member Governing Board; decisions
   quarterly (Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) — low-frequency, so each carries outsized signal.
3. **Hierarchy:** quarterly assessment > presser > speeches; sight-deposit data (weekly)
   is the *revealed* intervention signal between meetings. 4. **Variables:** CHF nominal
   TWI ("highly valued" language ladder), imported inflation. 5. **People:** verify board.
6. **Instruments:** policy rate (verify ticker), EURCHF, sight deposits (snb.ch).
7. **Traps:** communication is terse German/French/English — the TWI adjective ladder
   ("highly valued" vs. removals) is the signal; intervention is deniable, watch sight
   deposits not words. 8. **Always live:** rate, TWI language, sight-deposit trend.

## BoC (Bank of Canada)
1. **Mandate:** 2% CPI target (1–3% band), renewed ~5-yearly with the government
   `[verify current renewal]`. 2. **Mechanics:** Governing Council (~6), consensus, **no
   published votes**. 8 fixed announcement dates/yr. 3. **Hierarchy:** statement > MPR +
   presser (quarterly) > deliberation summaries (post-2023 innovation) > speeches;
   deputy-governor "economic progress report" speeches after non-MPR meetings are the
   designed signal slot. 4. **Variables:** core measures (trim/median), housing/household
   debt sensitivity (high pass-through economy), CAD, US spillovers. 5. **People:**
   verify. 6. **Instruments:** CORRA futures (verify root), CAD OIS via WIRP (verify),
   `USDCAD Curncy`. 7. **Traps:** no votes → speeches over-weighted; mortgage-renewal
   mechanics make policy bite faster than in the US — don't map Fed lags onto Canada.
8. **Always live:** rate, QT status, council roster.

## RBA (Reserve Bank of Australia)
1. **Mandate:** CPI 2–3% over time + full employment; post-2023 review structure with a
   separate Monetary Policy Board `[verify composition/vote publication status]`.
2. **Mechanics:** 8 meetings/yr (post-reform), statement + presser each meeting.
3. **Hierarchy:** statement/presser > SoMP (quarterly) > minutes > speeches.
4. **Variables:** trimmed-mean CPI (the target measure in practice), wage price index,
   labor underutilization, China/commodity terms of trade, household variable-rate debt
   (fast transmission). 5. **People:** verify. 6. **Instruments:** ASX 30-day interbank
   cash rate futures (verify BBG root), AUD OIS, `AUDUSD Curncy`, iron ore as the terms-
   of-trade shadow input. 7. **Traps:** trimmed-mean vs. headline divergence around
   energy/rent; quarterly CPI (monthly indicator partial `[verify status]`) makes the
   RBA data-sparse between quarters — speeches fill the void, overreact less.
8. **Always live:** cash rate, board roster, vote publication rules.

## RBNZ (Reserve Bank of New Zealand)
1. **Mandate:** 1–3% CPI midpoint focus; remit set by government — **check current remit**,
   employment leg was added then removed by governments `[verify]`. 2. **Mechanics:** MPC
   with external members; 7 policy dates/yr; publishes an **OCR projection track** —
   rare and precious. 3. **Hierarchy:** MPS (quarterly, includes OCR track) > reviews >
   speeches. 4. **Variables:** non-tradables CPI, inflation expectations surveys, net
   migration swings, dairy terms of trade. 5. **People:** verify. 6. **Instruments:**
   NZD OIS (verify), `NZDUSD Curncy`, bank-bill futures (verify root). 7. **Traps:** the
   OCR track is a *projection not commitment* but the market trades it as guidance —
   the delta in the track is the release's true headline; RBNZ has a maverick tradition,
   expect larger surprises than G4 base rates suggest. 8. **Always live:** OCR, current
   remit text, OCR track vintage.

## PBoC (People's Bank of China)
1. **Mandate:** multi-objective (growth, employment, price stability, FX stability,
   financial stability) under State Council direction — **not independent**; read policy
   through the Party/State Council fiscal-monetary joint lens. 2. **Mechanics:** no
   Western-style vote/meeting cycle; policy arrives via instrument moves (7d reverse-repo
   rate — the de facto policy rate `[verify]`, MLF, LPR monthly fixings, RRR cuts) and
   quarterly Monetary Policy Reports. 3. **Hierarchy:** instrument action > State
   Council/Politburo readouts > MPR (esp. the column boxes) > governor pressers > PBoC-
   adjacent media (Financial News/金融时报 front pages pre-signal). 4. **Variables:**
   credit growth (TSF/aggregate financing), CNY fixing vs. counter-cyclical factor,
   property-sector stress, deflation risk. 5. **People:** verify governor and Party
   secretary (may differ — the Party role outranks). 6. **Instruments:** daily CNY fixing
   (`USDCNY` mid vs. survey — the fixing *deviation* is the FX-policy signal), 7d repo
   `[verify ticker]`, LPR 1y/5y, RRR announcements. 7. **Traps:** translation + boilerplate
   decoding — stock phrases ("prudent monetary policy," "flexible and appropriate")
   carry calibrated meanings that shift by modifier; deletions of stock phrases are the
   signal. Never read PBoC statements without the prior version side by side. 8. **Always
   live:** every rate (RR/MLF/LPR/RRR), fixing behavior, latest MPR, leadership.

## Riksbank & Norges Bank (Nordics)
1. **Mandates:** Riksbank 2% CPIF; Norges 2% CPI with flexible inflation targeting.
2. **Mechanics:** small boards; Norges publishes a **full policy-rate path** (like RBNZ),
   Riksbank likewise publishes a rate path `[verify current practice]` — path deltas are
   the headline. 3. **Hierarchy:** MPR + path > presser > minutes (attributed — Riksbank
   minutes name names, unusually rich) > speeches. 4. **Variables:** Riksbank — CPIF,
   SEK weakness pass-through, household floating-rate debt; Norges — oil-sector spillovers,
   NOK, wage settlements. 5. **People:** verify. 6. **Instruments:** SEK/NOK OIS (verify),
   `EURSEK`, `EURNOK Curncy`, Brent for Norges context. 7. **Traps:** small-currency
   liquidity makes pricing jumpy — Axis 2 comparisons need wider error bars; English
   translations are good but pressers are local-language first. 8. **Always live:** rates,
   path vintages, board rosters.
