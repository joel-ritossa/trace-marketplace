# CB MODULE — EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK (Governing Council)

*Durable structure only. §8 always live. Personnel entries dated `[verify]`.*

## 1. Mandate & framework
Primary mandate: price stability — 2% HICP symmetric over the medium term (2021 strategy
review; a subsequent review assessment `[verify]`). Secondary objectives (employment,
environment) subordinate. No employment mandate equivalence with the Fed — don't import
Fed logic. Policy rates: deposit facility rate (DFR — the operative rate in the current
regime), MRO, marginal lending. Balance-sheet tools historically: APP/PEPP (run-off
status `[verify]`), TLTROs, TPI (anti-fragmentation backstop, never yet used `[verify]`).

## 2. Committee mechanics
Governing Council = 6 Executive Board members (always vote) + 20 national central bank
governors under a **rotation system**: governors share 15 votes (top-5 economies share 4
votes; the rest share 11). In practice decisions are by consensus and **votes are not
published** — dissent surfaces through speeches and sourced press, not vote counts.
Meetings every ~6 weeks; quiet period 7 days before.

## 3. Communication hierarchy (weight, descending)
1. Policy statement + staff projections (quarterly: Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) — projections drive.
2. President's presser (45 min after statement) — including the opening statement diff.
3. Monetary policy accounts (~4 weeks later) — unattributed; read for the *balance* of
   views, not individuals.
4. Executive Board speeches — esp. the chief-economist seat (traditionally frames the
   policy proposal to the GC) `[verify current holder]`.
5. National governor speeches — weight by economy size and by known proximity to the
   board's center; hawk/dove wings are structurally persistent (northern creditor blocs
   hawkish, southern doves) but individuals `[verify]`.
6. Sourced stories (Reuters/Bloomberg "ECB insiders") — the ECB leaks by tradition;
   sourced pieces within days of a meeting are quasi-official trial balloons.

## 4. Current-priority variables `[verify each use]`
HICP core and services momentum (3m/3m SAAR), negotiated wages, compensation per
employee, profit-margin absorption, TPI-relevant sovereign spreads (BTP-Bund), euro TWI
pass-through. Fragmentation risk is a standing constraint the Fed doesn't have.

## 5. People — ALL entries `[verify before use]`
President, VP, chief economist, and the hawk/dove map: pull current from
ecb.europa.eu/ecb/orga/decisions/govc. Long-cited national leanings (e.g., Bundesbank
institutional hawkishness) are durable enough to cite with a date; individuals aren't.

## 7. Known traps
- **Language:** presser and statements are in English (canonical); many governor
  speeches are in national languages — check whether you're reading a translation, and
  flag idiom-level readings as low-confidence.
- Consensus choreography: the presser line "some members would have preferred…" is the
  published form of a dissent — weight it like a vote count.
- The accounts are sanitized and lag; they confirm, rarely reveal.
- Projection-round meetings (quarterly) carry the policy action; interim meetings rarely
  surprise — calibrate expectations by meeting type.
- Sourced stories sometimes fly trial balloons that are then walked back — direction
  signal, timing noise.

## 8. STALE BOUNDARY — always pull live
Current DFR/MRO; balance-sheet run-off status; current board membership and chief
economist; the current hawk/dove map; latest staff projections; latest statement
language; TPI/PEPP flexibility status. Sources: ecb.europa.eu (all artifacts), BBG ECO.
