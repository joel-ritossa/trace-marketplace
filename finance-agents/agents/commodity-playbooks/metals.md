## LME mechanics (the base-metals operating system)

Date structure: rolling prompt dates, the **3M** convention, and **cash-3M** as the
tightness gauge (backwardation = nearby scarcity); **tom-next** spikes are the squeeze
tell. Inventory craft: **on-warrant vs. cancelled warrants** — cancellations lead
physical withdrawals (someone claimed the metal); watch warrant concentration/dominant-
position reports (LME publishes holder bands) for squeeze setups `[recall — durable]`.

treat exchange-stock draws skeptically when premia don't confirm. Post-2022 nickel

## Copper (the one with a PhD)

**Physical dashboard:** the three-exchange stock picture (LME + SHFE + COMEX
*together*), **Yangshan import premium** (China's physical appetite — the honest
demand line vs. SHFE speculation (verify current source)), **TC/RCs** (smelter
treatment charges: *falling* TCs = concentrate scarcity = mine supply tight — a
supply signal 6–12m ahead of refined balances `[recall — durable]`), scrap spreads
(wide discount = scrap abundant = refined demand overstated).
**Arb craft:** LME-SHFE import arb must be computed VAT- and duty-adjusted — the
open/closed arb window explains most short-term stock migrations; don't read
migration as demand.
**Demand decomposition:** grid + construction + appliances + transport (China ~half of
demand `[recall — durable]`); the China property pipeline (completions, not starts —
copper is late-stage wiring) + grid capex + EV content. Route from agent 05's China
module: credit impulse leads copper ~6–12m.
**Craft:** copper trades as China macro proxy *except* when its own physical dashboard
disagrees loudly — those divergences (macro selling + falling TCs + backwardation)
are the highest-conviction setups in the complex.

## Aluminum / zinc / nickel (energy in solid form)

Aluminum: power is roughly half of smelting cash cost `[recall — durable, verify
share]` — the cost curve *moves with energy markets*; smelter restarts/curtailments
are the supply elasticity. Premia (Midwest, Rotterdam — paid *over* LME) carry the
regional physical signal and the tariff story separately from the LME price: always
quote the all-in price. Zinc: mine-vs-smelter bottleneck via TCs (same logic as
copper). Nickel: class-1 vs. class-2 (NPI) split — the LME contract prices class-1,
Indonesian NPI supply floods class-2; the spread between them is the real market
`[recall — durable]`.

## Gold (a macro asset wearing a commodity costume)

**Model:** gold vs. **real rates** (10y TIPS) is the anchor regression — but the
*residual* is the trade: a persistent gap vs. the real-rate model = the central-bank/
de-dollarization bid (quantify via WGC quarterly CB purchases) or positioning froth.
Track which regime the residual is in; never trade gold off real rates alone when the
residual has trended one way for months.
**Flow hierarchy:** central banks (structural, price-insensitive) > ETF holdings
(trend-following retail/institutional) > COMEX managed money (fast) > OTC/physical
premia (India/China import premia = price-sensitive demand floor). Silver: gold's
high-beta sibling + industrial (solar) kicker; the ratio is a risk-appetite read.
**Craft:** gold breakouts on *falling* real rates are trend; breakouts *against*
rising real rates are regime information (someone structural is buying) — the second
kind matters more.
