## Software / SaaS
**KPIs:** NRR and **GRR separately** (NRR hides churn under expansion; get the gross
number), definition audited (timing of contraction recognition, whether multi-year
ramps count); net-new ARR *sequential* (the honest growth line once base is large);
**cRPO growth vs. revenue growth** (forward truth vs. reported past); CAC payback in
gross-profit months; Rule of 40 *including* SBC; seats vs. consumption split
(consumption models are macro-cyclical, seat models lag via headcount).
**Valuation:** EV/ARR or EV/gross profit adjusted for growth+margin; terminal FCF
margin argued from unit economics, not asserted.
**Traps:** billings duration games; capitalized commissions; "one-time" deals inside
NRR; SBC excluded from "profitability."
**Questions:** gross logo retention by cohort; sales productivity (attainment %,
ramped-rep count); % of ARR from the top product (concentration of the growth story).

## Semiconductors & semicap
**KPIs:** book-to-bill, lead times (stretching = double-ordering risk; compressing =
air pocket), inventory at customers AND channel (the cycle is an inventory phenomenon),
utilization, pricing (memory spot vs. contract spread), WFE spend vs. guidance
(semicap), services/backlog attach.
**Cycle law:** orders overshoot demand both directions — every cycle. "Secular, not
cyclical" claimed at peak margins is the top-tick bell `[recall — durable]`.
**Valuation:** through-cycle earnings power, not peak EPS × peak multiple; memory on
P/B vs. cycle position.
**Traps:** shipping into channel ahead of demand; "design wins" as revenue promises;
export-control exposure buried in geographic footnotes.
**Questions:** where are customer inventories vs. normal, in weeks; pricing next
quarter, not last.

## Internet / platforms
**KPIs:** engagement (DAU/MAU and time — honesty varies; prefer third-party
triangulation), ARPU by geography (mix shifts masquerade as growth), **take rate and
its ceiling** (every marketplace thesis dies or lives here), incremental margins on
ads vs. other revenue, capex intensity cycle (infra build vs. harvest).
**Traps:** metric retirement (a disclosed KPI that disappears was about to inflect
down); "adjusted" user definitions; contra-revenue (incentives netted vs. gross).
**Questions:** organic vs. paid user mix; pricing power evidence (has a take-rate
increase been absorbed?); regulatory exposure by revenue line.

## Banks
**KPIs:** NIM decomposed — asset repricing vs. **deposit beta and mix** (NIB deposit
runoff is the funding story); loan growth *by category with spreads*; credit: NCOs
vs. provisions vs. reserve ratio (CECL front-loads — releases flatter EPS later);
efficiency ratio; CET1 vs. requirement and the real payout capacity; **AOCI and HTM
marks** (the 2023 lesson: mark the whole book yourself).
**Valuation:** P/TBV regressed on ROTE across the peer set — buy the residual, not
the multiple.
**Traps:** reserve releases as "earnings"; securities restructurings that crystallize
then re-risk; CRE granularity (office vs. the rest — demand the split).
**Questions:** deposit beta assumption in the NII guide; unrealized loss migration;
CRE maturity wall by year.

## Insurance
**P&C KPIs:** combined ratio decomposed — underlying loss vs. expense vs. **cat load
vs. prior-year development** (persistent favorable PYD = conservative reserving =
quality; adverse PYD = the classic blow-up path); written rate vs. loss-cost trend
(the margin *direction* regardless of today's CR); float growth and cost.
**Life:** assumption-review season is the earnings event; statutory vs. GAAP capital
divergence. Complexity discount deserved — underwrite only with an edge.
**Valuation:** P/B vs. ROE, cat-normalized.
**Questions:** reserve triangle trends by line; rate adequacy by state/line; reinsurance
attachment changes.

## REITs
**KPIs:** **AFFO, not FFO** (FFO ignores maintenance capex — the honesty gap between
them is the management-quality read); same-store NOI growth split rate vs. occupancy;
cap-rate spread to marginal debt cost (accretion math); debt ladder and
variable/fixed mix; NAV by subsector cap rates.
**Subsector law:** towers/data centers/industrial/self-storage/office/retail/resi are
different businesses sharing a wrapper — never cross-compare multiples without the
growth/capex profile.
**Traps:** FFO add-backs of recurring costs; development "yield-on-cost" on
un-risk-adjusted assumptions; ground leases buried.
**Questions:** releasing spreads; tenant credit watchlist; development pipeline
funding source (equity issuance dilution vs. retained).

## Energy (E&P / refiners / services)
**E&P KPIs:** breakeven by basin honestly loaded (full-cycle incl. land/G&A, not
half-cycle well math); decline rate → maintenance capex → **true FCF at strip**; hedge
book (% hedged, at what, collars vs. swaps); reserve replacement and PUD conversion;
capital-returns framework credibility.
**Refiners:** crack capture rate vs. benchmark (the operations read), complexity,
turnaround calendar, RIN/regulatory costs, product yield flexibility.
**Services:** dayrates/pricing vs. utilization (pricing leads), backlog quality,
international vs. NAM mix.
**Traps:** "capital discipline" claims — audit against actual capex revisions;
half-cycle IRR marketing. Route macro (curve, cracks, OPEC) to agent 04 — the equity
call is capture + allocation, the commodity call is 04's.
**Questions:** sustaining capex at flat production; hedge policy at cycle turns;
acreage inventory life at current pace.

## Metals & mining
**KPIs:** position on the cost curve via **AISC honestly computed** (by-product credits
can make a bad mine look cheap — check co-product accounting); grade trajectory
(reserve grade vs. mined grade — high-grading steals from the future); capex phase
(builder vs. harvester); jurisdiction risk weighting; net debt through the cycle.
**Traps:** feasibility-study inflation (development names); "measured & indicated"
traded as reserves; royalty/streaming obligations buried.
**Valuation:** P/NAV at conservative long-run prices + spot-price torque stated
separately; EV/EBITDA misleads across capex phases.
**Questions:** cost guidance FX/energy assumptions; permitting critical path; what
spot price is management's budget assuming (vs. agent 04's view).

## Industrials / machinery / multis
**KPIs:** orders, backlog, book-to-bill by segment (the cycle position); **price/cost
spread** (pricing carried vs. input inflation — the margin bridge every quarter);
aftermarket/services mix (the margin ballast and the moat); short- vs. long-cycle
revenue mix; distributor destocking phase.
**Traps:** roll-ups: organic growth disclosure games (acquisition revenue in "organic"
after 12 months — check the definition); percentage-of-completion on megaprojects
(claims/unapproved change orders in contract assets); "restructuring" as a permanent
line item.
**Valuation:** EV/EBITA through-cycle; sum-of-parts only with a catalyst to close it.
**Questions:** backlog margin vs. P&L margin (the forward mix); channel inventory in
weeks; incremental margin guidance by segment.

## Transports
**KPIs:** rails — OR (operating ratio) with the mix caveat, volumes by commodity
weekly (public data — a macro tell beyond the position), service metrics
(velocity/dwell — degrade before margins do); truckload — spot vs. contract spread
(the cycle clock: spot below contract = capacity exiting → tighten coming); airlines
— RASM/CASM-ex, capacity discipline (watch scheduled capacity data, not claims);
parcel — volume/yield trade-off.
**Traps:** fuel surcharges flattering yields; "structural" OR improvements that are
mix; airline "capacity discipline" lasting until the first market-share loss.
**Use:** freight complex doubles as a real-time macro input — route weekly rail/truck
data to agent 10's growth composite.

## Consumer staples
**KPIs:** **organic growth split into volume/mix vs. price** — the entire post-2021
story lives in this split: price-driven "growth" with negative volumes is share donation
to private label in slow motion; gross margin vs. A&P reinvestment (cutting brand
spend to hold EPS = borrowing from the moat); market share by scanner data (request
Nielsen/Circana reads); private-label share trend in the category.
**Traps:** shipment-vs-consumption gaps (channel fill/drain around price increases);
FX-neutral guidance hiding transaction-FX margin hits; "premiumization" narratives at
the elasticity cliff.
**Questions:** elasticity realized vs. modeled by brand tier; promo depth/frequency
trend; retailer inventory weeks.

## Consumer discretionary / retail / restaurants
**KPIs:** SSS **split traffic vs. ticket** (ticket-driven comps with falling traffic
age badly); **inventory growth minus sales growth spread** (the single best markdown
predictor `[recall — durable]`); gross margin bridge (freight, shrink, mix, markdown);
new-unit economics (year-1 ROIC, payback, cannibalization) vs. the reinvestment rate —
the compounding engine or the treadmill; e-commerce margin honesty (fully loaded incl.
returns/logistics).
**Restaurants:** SSS vs. traffic, franchised vs. company mix (different valuation
animals), development pipeline net of closures, commodity basket exposure.
**Traps:** retail calendars (53rd week, holiday shifts); "unit growth" outrunning
returns; loyalty-program accounting changes juicing revenue.
**Questions:** traffic trend *this quarter to date*; markdown plan; new-unit cohort
performance vs. class of 2 years ago.

## Healthcare — pharma / biotech
**KPIs:** pipeline valued probability-adjusted **by phase and indication base rates**
(use published PoS tables (verify current source) — never management's enthusiasm);
LOE cliff map (revenue at risk by year, erosion curves small-molecule vs. biologic
differ sharply); gross-to-net bogey (list-price growth is not revenue growth);
US pricing-policy exposure by product (IRA negotiation lists etc. `[pull live]`).
**Biotech:** cash runway vs. catalyst calendar (dilution *before* the readout is the
pattern); trial-design forensics — endpoint switches, open-label bias, post-hoc
subgroups, single-arm comparisons to historical controls: each is a red flag with a
base rate.
**Valuation:** risk-adjusted NPV by asset + pipeline optionality; big pharma on P/E
only with the cliff map in hand.
**Questions:** enrollment pace (slippage predicts readout slippage); payer coverage
assumptions in guidance; the *next* trial's powering.

## Healthcare — services / managed care / devices
**Managed care KPIs:** MLR vs. guide (utilization surprise is the whole print);
membership mix (MA vs. commercial vs. Medicaid — margin and regulatory profiles
differ); MA rate notice + STAR ratings (the annuity's regulator-set coupon);
redetermination/enrollment policy flows `[pull live]`.
**Providers/services:** volumes (procedures/admissions), payer mix, labor cost trend
(contract vs. employed staffing ratio).
**Devices:** procedure-volume recovery/backlog, ASP pressure by category, salesforce
productivity, replacement-cycle timing.
**Traps:** utilization "normalization" claims each January; risk-adjustment coding
scrutiny (MA) `[pull status live]`.

## Utilities & infrastructure
**KPIs:** rate-base growth (the EPS algorithm: rate base × allowed ROE × payout),
**allowed vs. earned ROE gap** (regulatory lag = execution read), jurisdiction quality
(constructive vs. adversarial commissions — track outcomes, not adjectives), equity
needs (the growth capex that requires dilution), fuel/power cost pass-through
mechanics, load growth (data centers changed this input — verify per name).
**Traps:** "rate-base growth" funded by perpetual equity issuance (per-share math
first); holding-company leverage atop regulated opco; renewables development margins
assumed on uncontracted pipeline.
**Questions:** pending rate cases and asks; capex plan financing split; regulatory
calendar next 12 months.

## Media / telecom
**KPIs:** subs/ARPU/churn by segment with the saturation question asked honestly;
content spend vs. amortization policy (aggressive amortization curves flatter early
profits); telecom: capex-cycle position (fiber/spectrum builds → harvest), convergence
bundling economics, promotional intensity as the competitive thermometer.
**Traps:** "adjusted EBITDA" in content businesses (cash content spend vs. amort);
one-time sports/rights step-ups treated as recurring; churn masked by bundle
re-classification.
**Questions:** net adds *ex-promotional* cohorts; content ROI evidence; pricing-power
test results (last increase's churn effect).
