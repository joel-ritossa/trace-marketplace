# SECTOR MODEL — CONSUMER STAPLES (food · beverage · HPC · tobacco)

*Full model spec for Agents 01/09. The sector's entire modern story is in one split:
volume/mix vs. price.*

## 1. Model architecture

```
1. Organic growth BY REGION × CATEGORY = volume + mix + price (model all three;
   price-driven growth with negative volume is share donation in slow motion —
   the elasticity clock is running)
2. Volume drivers: category growth (population + consumption trends) × market share
   (scanner data trend) — separate the market from the share
3. GROSS MARGIN BRIDGE (quarterly): input basket (commodities from agent 04 with
   hedge/contract lags of 2–4 quarters) + freight + FX transaction + productivity
   programs + mix − promo depth
4. A&P (advertising & promotion) as a STRATEGY line, not a plug: cutting brand
   spend to protect EPS = borrowing from the moat — model it explicitly and flag
   sustained cuts as quality erosion
5. Below: FX translation schedule (guidance is usually FX-neutral — bridge to
   reported), bolt-on M&A vs. organic definition, buyback cadence
6. FCF: working-capital programs (supplier financing — check the footnote), capex
   ~sustaining levels
```
**Tobacco/beverage specifics:** pricing-power annuity models — volume decline rate ×
price escalation spread; next-gen product P&L separated (investment phase vs. core
annuity); excise-tax pass-through mechanics.

## 2. KPI dictionary

Organic growth split (volume/mix/price — the headline KPI); market share by scanner
(Nielsen/Circana (verify access)); private-label share trend in the category;
elasticity realized vs. modeled (volume response to price by brand tier); promo depth
and frequency vs. category; gross margin vs. A&P reinvestment pair; % of portfolio
gaining share; emerging-market volume growth (the structural growth argument).

## 3. Valuation framework

Relative P/E vs. its own 10y band and vs. staples peers adjusted for organic-growth
quality (volume-led deserves the premium; price-led doesn't — the market eventually
agrees); DCF with terminal growth disciplined by category growth + share position.
Reverse: implied organic growth × margin vs. the volume evidence. Staples re-rate on
*regime* (bond-proxy behavior — agent 10 Axis 4: in positive stock-bond-correlation
regimes the defensive premium compresses).

## 4. Earnings quality & forensics

Shipment vs. consumption gaps (channel fill before price increases, de-load after —
compare sell-in growth to scanner sell-through); "organic" definitions absorbing
acquisitions; supplier-finance programs stretching payables (the working-capital
mirage — disclosure now required, read it); promotional accruals timing; FX-neutral
guidance hiding transaction-cost margin hits; incessant "productivity program"
restructuring add-backs; capitalizing marketing as "customer relationships" post-M&A.

## 5. Cycle & macro dashboard

Input costs from agent 04 (ags/softs/energy/packaging) with the 2–4 quarter lag
schedule; consumer trade-down cycle (private-label share acceleration = the
squeeze tell — agent 05's real-income data leads it); pricing umbrella integrity
(when the category leader stops raising, the laggards' margin story dies); GLP-1
category effects where relevant `[pull current evidence — contested]`; EM FX for
translation-heavy names.

## 6. Data map

Scanner data summaries (Nielsen/Circana via sell-side or direct (verify access));
company organic-growth bridges (log definitions); commodity inputs via agent 04;
promo-tracking (verify vendor). BBG DAL consensus; `RV` peer band.

## 7. Thesis archetypes & management questions

**Longs:** volume inflection after elasticity reset (price laps, volumes recover,
margin structure intact) before consensus models it; share-gainer in a category the
market treats as commodity; EM compounder at DM-decliner multiple. **Shorts:**
price-only grower at the elasticity cliff with private label accelerating; A&P-cut
EPS quality about to mean-revert; supplier-finance unwind forcing working-capital
truth. **Questions:** volume/mix vs. price next 4 quarters by region; elasticity
observed this quarter by tier; promo plans vs. category; where private label sits in
their categories and the price-gap threshold; A&P as % of sales vs. 5 years ago and
the justification.
