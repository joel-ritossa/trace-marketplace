## Retail sales — Census, ~16th, 8:30 ET, monthly. Tier 1.

**Vector — Tier 1:** headline m/m (`RSAFS` (verify)); ex-autos; ex-autos-ex-gas;
**control group** = total ex autos, gas, building materials, food services — feeds
goods PCE directly; know the construction and compute it if the wire omits it.
**Vector — Tier 2 categories (each z-scored):** motor vehicles & parts (cross-check
against unit sales — dollars can rise on price alone); gasoline stations (a *price*
series in disguise — always deflate mentally with gas prices before reading demand);
building materials (weather-sensitive); food & beverage stores; **food services &
drinking places** (the only services line — the discretionary-spending canary
`[recall — durable]`); **nonstore retailers** (e-commerce; holiday SA quirks
Oct–Jan); general merchandise; furniture; electronics; clothing; health & personal
care; sporting goods/hobby; miscellaneous.
**Derived:** real control group (deflate with CPI goods — nominal retail in an
inflation regime systematically overstates volumes); discretionary basket (food
services + clothing + furniture + electronics) vs. staples basket.
**Reading:** retail is nominal, goods-heavy, and ~⅓ of consumption — never extrapolate
it to total consumer health without the services signal (income & outlays below).
Revisions are large; the annual benchmark (~April) can rewrite recent history — check
the revision row before trend claims.
**Reaction fn:** goods-PCE nowcast; consumer-strength leg of the growth composite
(agent 10).

## Industrial production & capacity utilization — Fed G.17, ~15th–17th, 9:15 ET. Tier 2.

**Vector:** headline IP (`INDPRO` verified); **manufacturing IP** (the signal — strip
utilities and mining first); utilities (weather noise — never interpret); mining
(≈ oil & gas activity — route to agent 04); by market group: consumer goods, business
equipment (the capex-realized read), construction supplies, materials; motor-vehicle
assemblies (verify id) — strip for ex-autos manufacturing trend. Capacity utilization
(`TCU` verified) — level vs. its own long-run mean; the CU→inflation link is weak in
the modern economy `[recall — durable]`, use as slack context only.
**Reading:** manufacturing IP ex-autos is the cleanest hard-data cross-check on ISM —
when surveys and IP disagree for 2+ months, believe IP for level, surveys for turn.
**Reaction fn:** growth leg; mining detail → agent 04 (rig/production trends).

## Durable goods / core capex — Census, ~26th, 8:30 ET, monthly. Tier 2.

**Vector:** headline (`DGORDER` (verify)) — dominated by transportation lumps;
ex-transportation; defense strip; **core capex orders** = nondefense capital goods
ex-aircraft (`NEWORDER` (verify)) and **core capex shipments** (feeds GDP equipment
investment directly — the nowcast line); unfilled orders and inventories
(inventory-to-shipments ratio).
**Reading:** orders lead, shipments nowcast. Boeing months (large aircraft orders)
make the headline meaningless — always lead with core. Core capex in *real* terms
(deflate — capex deflators ran hot post-2021) before calling an investment cycle.
**Reaction fn:** business-investment leg of the growth composite; capex cycle input
for equity sector work (agents 01/02).

## Housing complex — starts/permits (Census, ~17th), NAHB (~16th), new/existing home sales, Case-Shiller. Tier 2 as a complex; the rates-transmission gauge.

**Vector:** starts and **permits** (`HOUST`, `PERMIT` verified) — permits lead starts,
single-family vs. multifamily **always split** (they cycle on different drivers:
single-family = rates/affordability; multifamily = credit + completed-supply
overhang); units under construction and completions (the supply actually arriving —
feeds rents ~with a lag); NAHB builder sentiment (leads single-family permits ~2m
`[recall — durable]`) with its buyer-traffic sub-index; new home sales (timely,
huge revisions, signed-contracts basis) vs. existing home sales (closings — lagging,
inventory months-of-supply is the price signal); mortgage applications (MBA, weekly —
the highest-frequency rates-transmission series); Case-Shiller (3m average, very
lagged — for confirmation only).
**Derived:** single-family permits 3m trend (the single best housing summary);
completions-minus-starts (pipeline draining/building → rent pressure direction —
route to the inflation module's shelter section).
**Reading:** housing is *the* monetary-policy transmission channel — read every print
as "is policy binding?" Multifamily completions surging while starts collapse is a
rent-disinflation pipeline, a 12–18m gift to the CPI shelter view; say so explicitly
when present.
**Reaction fn:** transmission evidence for agent 03; shelter pipeline for the
inflation dashboard; homebuilder/building-products read-throughs for agents 01/02.

## GDP — BEA, 8:30 ET, quarterly (advance/second/third). Tier 1 on advance, fading after.

**Vector (contributions to growth, pp — the release publishes them; z-score the
contributions, not just the headline):** consumption (goods/services split);
equipment; intellectual property; structures; **residential**; **inventories** (noise
— never in the signal column); **net exports** (noise — often the mirror of
inventories); government (federal/state-local).
**Derived:** **final sales to private domestic purchasers** (core demand — the line
that matters; compute if the wire buries it); GDP-GDI gap when GDI available (large
gaps get revised toward GDI about as often as not `[recall — durable caveat]` — treat
the gap as uncertainty, not signal); the deflator internals vs. the CPI/PCE complex.
**Reading:** the advance print misses the final month of trade/inventories data —
those two components are BEA assumptions until the second estimate; discount
surprises driven by them. Annual benchmark revisions (Jul) can rewrite the narrative
— re-run the trend claims after each.
**Reaction fn:** the growth composite's anchor (agent 10); GDPNow-vs-actual gap
closes the nowcast loop.

## Personal income & outlays — BEA, monthly (same release as PCE deflator). Tier 2, underrated.

**Vector:** real disposable income; real spending (goods/services); **saving rate**
(revision-prone — never over-read one month); wages & salaries component (the
BEA-side cross-check on the payroll income proxy); real spending 3m ann. vs. real
income 3m ann.
**Reading:** spending running ahead of income = drawdown of buffers, self-limiting;
the income side (payrolls × hours × wages, cross-checked here) is the sustainable
core of the consumer call. This section, not retail sales, is where the consumer
verdict lives; retail is the timely proxy, this is the truth.
**Reaction fn:** consumption leg of growth composite; saving-rate trend into the
recession-risk assessment.
