## University of Michigan — prelim ~2nd Fri, final ~4th Fri, 10:00 ET. Tier 2 (Tier 1 lines: the inflation expectations).

**Vector:** headline sentiment; **current conditions vs. expectations split** (the
expectations-minus-current gap is the cycle signal, not the level); **1-year inflation
expectations**; **5–10 year inflation expectations** (the line the Fed cites — a 0.3
move here outranks everything else in the release); buying conditions for
durables/vehicles/homes ("good time to buy" — rates-sensitivity read).
**Reading:**
1. The 5–10y series is the anchoring gauge: stable = everything else is noise; a
   sustained break above its long-range band is a policy-relevant event — flag to
   agent 03 immediately.
2. **Partisan gap caveat (structural):** expectations split hard by political
   affiliation and swing on elections, not economics — read the by-party detail
   (published) before calling a shift genuine `[recall — durable]`.
3. Methodology moved to web interviews (mid-2020s) with a level shift in some series
   (verify the splice date before long-history z-scores — tell §3 to shorten the
   window if unspliced).
4. Prelim→final revisions are non-trivial; the prelim is the market event.
**Reaction fn:** inflation-expectations leg of the inflation dashboard; rates-
transmission color from buying conditions.

## Conference Board Consumer Confidence — last Tue, 10:00 ET. Tier 2.

**Vector:** headline; present situation vs. expectations split (expectations <80 is
the Board's own recession flag — cite as theirs, not gospel); **labor differential**
= jobs plentiful − jobs hard-to-get (the release's best line — tracks U3 turns and
leads it slightly `[recall — durable]`); 12-month inflation expectations (noisier than
UMich's, directional use only); buying plans (autos/homes/appliances).
**Reading:** CB's panel skews older/higher-income vs. UMich — labor-market questions
are its comparative advantage, inflation questions are UMich's. When labor
differential and JOLTS quits both roll, weight the labor-cooling call up a notch.
**Reaction fn:** labor leg cross-check; expectations split into the recession-risk
assessment (agent 10).
